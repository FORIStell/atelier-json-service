      Tips

      • Fit one problem inside the frame. Drag the corners to resize it.

      • The calculator has templates for fractions, powers, roots, logs, limits, derivatives, integrals and sums. Hold a key with a red dot for more options.
      • Words work too: factor x^2-9, 15% of 80, area of circle radius 5.
