      Tips

      • Fit one problem inside the frame. Drag the corners to resize it.
      • On a picture, screenshot or shared screen: circle the problem you want solved.
      • On a computer: press Ctrl+V to paste a screenshot, or share your screen.
      • Use dark ink on light paper and good light. Hold the phone flat above the page.
      • Check what was read: tap Edit on the solution to fix any symbol.
      • The calculator has templates for fractions, powers, roots, logs, limits, derivatives, integrals and sums. Hold a key with a red dot for more options.
      • Words work too: factor x^2-9, 15% of 80, area of circle radius 5.
